What these mean. total is the full wake-to-reply round-trip. listen is how long the mic was open recording your speech. stt is Whisper turning that audio into text (song-recognition capture lands in action). llm is the Gemini API round-trip (sums across multiple calls if a tool was invoked). action is work the handler did itself — running a tool, capturing audio for song ID, etc. synth is Piper generating the audio for the reply. speaking is how long Orion actually talks out loud — not slowness, just length.

p95 = the 95th-percentile latency. 95% of turns were faster than this; only the slowest 5% were slower. Way more useful than average, which a couple of slow song lookups can drag up.
